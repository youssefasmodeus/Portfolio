##  Portfolio Guide: What You'll Find Here

This portfolio is a curated collection of my engineering work, ranging from university research to industry-inspired systems and personal R&D. To help you navigate, the repositories are generally structured around three core disciplines:

### 1. Robotics & Embedded Control
Repositories in this category focus on physical actuation and intelligent movement. 
* **What to expect:** You will find complete implementations of forward and inverse kinematics, real-time embedded control using C++, sensor fusion, and closed-loop hardware systems. These projects demonstrate my ability to translate mathematical motion planning into physical, vision-guided, and IoT-connected hardware.

### 2. System Simulation & Digital Twins
Repositories here showcase my expertise in Model-Based Design and system validation. 
* **What to expect:** These projects feature high-fidelity physical plant models built in MATLAB/Simulink and Simscape. They demonstrate my ability to reverse-engineer complex industrial processes, validate algorithms against simulated physics, and design deterministic control architectures that isolate and correct system errors.

### 3. Machine Learning & Signal Processing
These repositories highlight the intersection of data and physical machinery.
* **What to expect:** You will find end-to-end AI pipelines designed for industrial applications. These projects emphasize robust data preprocessing (like sliding windows and signal filtering) and the deployment of both lightweight Edge ML models and deep learning architectures to extract meaningful patterns from noisy, imbalanced sensor data.
